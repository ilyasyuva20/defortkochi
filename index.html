Welcome to DE FORT KOCHI

Based in the heart of historic Kochi, DE FORT KOCHI offers immersive and authentic local experiences designed to showcase the vibrant culture, rich heritage, and scenic beauty of Fort Kochi.

We specialize in guided Heritage Walking Tours through picturesque lanes, colonial landmarks, bustling spice markets, and unforgettable Tuk-Tuk city rides.

Every journey is led by passionate local guides who share fascinating stories, history, and hidden gems that make every visit memorable.

Must Visit Places

Featured Heritage Attractions

Chinese Fishing Nets

Iconic cantilevered wooden nets dipped into the calm waterfront waters since the 14th century.

St Francis Church

The oldest European-built church in India (1503), originally burial site of explorer Vasco da Gama.

Jew Town & Synagogue

Bustling narrow streets filled with antique shops, aromatic spice warehouses, and historical charm.

Mattancherry Palace

Colonial Dutch Palace renowned for intricate mythology murals and royal heritage artifacts.
